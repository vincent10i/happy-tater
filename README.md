# Blob Feast

A fun browser game where you eat food, get fatter, and try to become the biggest blob alive!

## How to Play

- **Arrow keys** or **WASD** to move
- Eat food (emoji items) to gain weight and grow bigger
- Avoid the red spiky hazards — they'll pop you!
- Get big enough and you can eat the hazards too
- Level up every 15 foods eaten — unlocks bigger meals

## Features

- Jiggly, animated blob character that grows as you eat
- 14 different food types across multiple levels
- Dynamic difficulty — more hazards and faster enemies at higher levels
- Particle effects and floating weight gain indicators
- Your blob gets blushing cheeks and displays its weight when large enough

## Run Locally

Just open `index.html` in any modern browser. No build step needed.
